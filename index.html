Draft Topic 01 · Planning

Defining a Realistic Property Budget in East Delhi

Many first-time buyers budget solely for the headline sale price. Factor in registration fees, stamp duty, transfer documentation, and basic interior updates to prevent financial strain during transaction closing.

Key Takeaway: Maintaining a 10–12% contingency buffer ensures smooth deed execution.
Due Diligence
Draft Topic 02 · Inspection

Essential Checks Before Your First Property Visit

Before falling in love with finishes, examine the structural foundations: approach road width, parking feasibility, municipal water supply timings, electrical load capacity, and natural cross-ventilation.

Key Takeaway: An organized checklist inspection saves weeks of subsequent clarification.
Seller Advice
Draft Topic 03 · Selling

Preparing Your Property for a Smooth Sale

Serious buyers appreciate organized paperwork: title deed chain, property tax receipts, electricity clearance, and clear floor dimensions. Clear documentation shortens negotiation cycles substantially.

Key Takeaway: Paperwork readiness directly accelerates buyer commitment.
